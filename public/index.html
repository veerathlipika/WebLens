Important limitation

Passive does not mean complete.

WebLens deliberately does not attempt login bypass, privilege escalation, SQL injection, brute force, credential testing, exploitation, or destructive actions. Authentication and authorization require authorized, context-aware testing. The report tells you exactly what was observable and what should be tested next.
